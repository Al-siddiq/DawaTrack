# How to check cash and stock

Open **Cash check** to count the cash in the shop. Open **Stock check** to count items. Open **Cash and stock check** to do both together.

1. Choose the people who worked in the shop if you know them. Times can stay empty if you do not know them.
2. Press Start. Read the message first: work that changes the cash or stock being counted must wait until you finish or cancel this check.
3. Check each shop phone or computer on the list. If one cannot be used, the owner must give a reason to skip it.
4. Count the cash or items yourself. Enter what you counted and press Save. Enter **0** and save if there is none. Check every saved number.
5. Confirm the counts when they are right. You cannot change them after this. Someone allowed to check the numbers can then compare them with the shop records.
6. If the numbers differ, count again and save a reason. A difference does not tell us who caused it. Finish when every difference has a reason.

Finishing keeps the result. It does not add or remove money. Stock also stays as it is. If the owner chooses to change the stock record later, the page shows what will change and asks again before saving. Sales made after the count stay in the records.

If a reply is missing, press **Check what was saved**. Keep the same check number. Do not start another check to replace it. Reconnecting will not save it again for you.

If you must stop, give a reason and press Cancel. Counts already saved stay in the old check, but they do not become a finished count. Entries you have not saved are not kept. If DawaTrack says the check cannot continue after a system change, ask the owner to cancel it and start again.

You can add a note later if you learn more. A note does not change the saved numbers. If a button is missing or cannot be pressed, read the message or ask the owner for help.
