# DawaTrack wording

The owner requires plain English for ordinary Nigerian traders, including people with primary school education. This applies to new work and changes to existing screens.

- Every page should make clear where the person is, what they can do and what to do next.
- Use short sentences and familiar action words. Buttons should say what happens when pressed.
- Do not put accounting terms or developer terms in shop screens. Use “check”, “save”, “confirm”, “finish”, “cancel” and “difference”. Avoid “commit”, “frozen result”, “variance”, “scope”, “finalization” and “abort”.
- Explain what a choice does to cash, stock and saved work. Simple words must still tell the truth about permissions, lost replies and actions that cannot be undone.
- Keep technical names in code, APIs and schema where needed. Do not expose raw status codes, role names or internal field names as page labels.
- Follow [the writing guide](docs/ui/plain-english.md). Build tracked public assets from their source files.
